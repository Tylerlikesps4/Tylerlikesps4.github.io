> <cite>— my Twitch bio</cite>

Hi, I'm **Anu**, also **Tylerlikesps4** around the internet. I make games, little apps, videos and streams.

**Games.** I'm making [Harrow's Wake](/games/harrows-wake/), a PS1-style survival horror game in Godot about two survivors who can never meet and a bell-ringing stalker you can't kill. The free demo is out, and the code is open source. I also made [Last Toll](/games/last-toll/), a short first-person spin-off, in a week for a game jam. It's a love letter to late-'90s horror like Silent Hill, Clock Tower, Resident Evil and Parasite Eve: fog, fixed cameras, typewriter saves, and something you *hear* before you see.

**Apps.** I build small Android apps for my own life and my channels: [Anu Keystone](/apps/#keystone), [Pocketly](/apps/#pocketly), and [Anu Studio Crew](/apps/#studio-crew), which is coming soon. They're open source, work offline, and don't track you.

**Videos and streams.** I post on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@godkingdarkness0115) and stream on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/tylerlikesps4). See the [schedule](/videos/).

**When I'm not building,** I'm usually playing story-driven detective and narrative games.

**How I work.** I build in public, write devlogs, and use AI tools as an assistant. I say so on every project where it applies, and I credit every asset.
