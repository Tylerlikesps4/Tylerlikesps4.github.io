The best ways to reach me:

- **Found a bug in a game or app?** Open an issue on its GitHub repo: [Harrow's Wake](https://github.com/Tylerlikesps4/Harrows-Wake/issues), [Last Toll](https://github.com/Tylerlikesps4/Harrows-Wake-Last-Toll/issues), [Anu Keystone](https://github.com/Tylerlikesps4/Anu-Keystone/issues) or [Pocketly](https://github.com/Tylerlikesps4/Pocketly---Your-personal-budget/issues). Say what you were doing, what device you were on and which build.
- **Want to contribute code?** Pull requests are welcome. Each repo's README explains the rules.
- **Feedback on a game?** Leave a comment on its [itch.io](https://merlin-direwolves.itch.io) page.
- **Just want to talk?** Come say hi in [Twitch chat](https://www.twitch.tv/tylerlikesps4) or in the comments on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@godkingdarkness0115).
- **Everything else:** [github.com/Tylerlikesps4](https://github.com/Tylerlikesps4).

I don't take messages by email through this site.
