## The game

**St. Agnes Orphanage, 3:12 AM.** The children are gone. The Bellman is not.

He walks the halls every night, ringing his little hand bell. You're trapped inside with him, and the only way out is to find the **bell rope**, the **bell clapper** and the **chapel key**, unlock the chapel and ring the great bell before he finds you.

- **You can't fight him.** Listen for his bell and keep your distance.
- **Running is loud.** He hears it. Sneak when he's near.
- **Hide** in wardrobes or under a bed, but only if he didn't see you get in. If he did, he'll open the doors.
- **Hold your breath** when he's right outside.
- **Through walls his bell sounds muffled,** and its tempo tells you what he's doing: slow while he wanders, quicker when he's heard something, frantic in a chase.

Five lore notes, a few scares, an original music-box theme, and a win screen with your time, notes found, times you hid and times he saw you.

It's a one-night spin-off of [Harrow's Wake](/games/harrows-wake/), made from scratch during the jam week. Play it with headphones, in the dark.

## Controls

| | Keyboard + mouse | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrows | Left stick | Drag left half |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick | Drag right half |
| Run (loud) | Shift | B / L3 | RUN |
| Sneak / hold breath | Ctrl / C | R3 | BREATH (while hiding) |
| Use / hide | E / Space / Enter | A | USE |
| Flashlight | F | LB | LIGHT |
| Pause | Esc / P | Start | II |
