## The game

**Harrow's Wake, Maine. October 1998.** A logging town is drowning in fog, and under it sits a sealed biotech lab that dug up something older than the Earth. You hear the Bellman's hand bell before you see him. He can't be killed, only escaped.

In the demo you play two survivors who can never meet. **Jenny** hides from the Bellman in the St. Agnes orphanage with no weapon at all: she sneaks, listens for his bell, and hides in wardrobes, holding her breath when he's close. **Dana** is a deputy at the police station with a handgun and a flashlight. They keep each other alive by sending items through the town's old **pneumatic mail tubes**, and you switch between them at the typewriters, which are also where you save.

This is the **free, open-source demo**: one complete chapter with a beginning, middle and end. The full game is planned as a separate paid release.

### What's in the demo

- PSX look: vertex snapping, affine texture warping, 15-bit dither, CRT and film grain, fixed Resident Evil-style cameras.
- The Bellman: a stalker with a sight cone, hearing, chases and searches. Your flashlight lets him see you from further away, and if he sees you climb into a wardrobe, he'll open it.
- Hiding with a wardrobe-slit view, a hold-your-breath meter and a heartbeat that rises with panic.
- Tank or modern controls, on-screen touch controls on phones, gamepad support.
- Six documents to find, an objective in the pause menu, and a ranked ending (S to D).
- An original soundtrack, "Harrow's Wake (Main Theme)", as an instrumental title loop and a vocal version with lullaby lyrics over the end credits.

Headphones recommended: the bell and footsteps are positional, so you can hear which way he's coming from.

## How to play

- **Windows:** unzip and run `HarrowsWake.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, click *More info*, then *Run anyway* (the build is unsigned).
- **Linux:** unzip and run the x86_64 file.
- **Android:** install `HarrowsWake.apk`. Uninstall any older build first.
- **F11** or **Alt+Enter** toggles fullscreen on PC.
